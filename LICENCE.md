## 🧩 About

**Watch Dogs 2 Companion** is a curated list of Watch Dogs 2 open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, minimap reader, objective tracker
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — loadout catalog, gadget manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — mission layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Mission Helpers
- Objective Tracker — follow mission steps from any checkpoint
- Minimap Reader — visualize points of interest and routes
- Replay Notes — record and review mission runs
- Playback Speed — adjust movement pace for study (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎨 Catalog & Cosmetics
- Outfit Catalog — browse clothing, colors, and skins
- Vehicle Notes — organize cars, bikes, and tuning presets
- Visibility Notes — improved interface clarity

### 🏋️ Sandbox Layout
- Free-Roam Notes — segment study across districts
- Gadget Control — manually switch equipped gadgets mid-run
- Route Buffer Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the sandbox
